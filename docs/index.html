Why NeuroLoad

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

A clean desktop experience and a pocket Android app — same engine, same taste.

One link is all it takes

Paste any video URL — NeuroLoad analyzes it instantly: title, channel, duration, thumbnail and license. Then pick a quality and go.

Blazing fast by default

Built on yt-dlp + aria2c: 16 parallel connections, multi-fragment downloading and ffmpeg merging — faster than your browser.

Every quality, even MP3

1080p · 720p · 480p · 360p — or extract pure MP3 audio with highest bitrate. Perfect for CC music and lectures.

Honest & transparent

Read this before you download a single file

NeuroLoad is a tool for downloading content you own or content licensed as Creative Commons — or with explicit permission from the author. Downloading arbitrary copyrighted videos violates YouTube's Terms of Service and, in most countries, copyright law. The apps are intentionally not on Google Play / the Microsoft Store, and that's deliberate. Use common sense: personal use, CC content, your own uploads — that's the deal.
